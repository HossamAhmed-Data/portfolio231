Project Title: Superstore Sales & Performance Intelligence

View Dashboard Full Size View GitHub Repository
1. Project Overview

An end-to-end business intelligence solution analyzing retail sales, profitability, regional trends, and customer shipping patterns.

2. Business Problem

The retail management team lacked visibility into loss-making product categories and regional profit leakages, leading to misallocated marketing budgets.

3. Objective

Identify primary drivers of low profitability, evaluate regional shipping delays, and highlight top revenue-generating products.

4. Dataset Details

Source: Superstore Sample Dataset | Rows: 9,994+ records | Main Columns: Order Date, Sales, Profit, Category, Region, Ship Mode.

5. Data Cleaning & Transformation

Handled missing values in shipping dates using Power Query, removed duplicate transaction logs, and standardized date-time formats for accurate temporal aggregations.

6. Data Modeling & Analysis

Implemented a robust Star Schema architecture connecting a Central Fact Table (Orders) with Dimension Tables (Customers, Products, Calendar, Geography). Authored advanced DAX measures for Year-to-Date (YTD) sales and profit margins.

7. Key Insights
  • Certain sub-categories (e.g., Tables) yielded negative profit margins despite high gross sales.
  • Standard Class shipping accounted for over 60% of total volume with seasonal surges during Q4.
8. Business Solution / Impact

Provided executive leadership with targeted recommendations to discontinue unprofitable sub-categories and optimize inventory distribution, directly supporting leaner operational planning.

9. Tools Used

Power BI Power Query DAX Excel
