Dashboard Case Study: Superstore Sales & Profitability Intelligence

View Dashboard Full Size View GitHub Repository
1. Executive Summary

An interactive BI reporting solution designed to help retail executives monitor sales performance, track regional profit leakages, and evaluate shipping efficiencies.

2. The Business Problem

The client's management team faced blind spots regarding loss-making product sub-categories. High gross sales volumes in certain regions were masking heavy discounting and negative profit margins.

3. Analytical Objective

Pinpoint primary drivers of profit erosion, analyze shipping mode performance across seasons, and provide clear visibility into top revenue-generating segments.

4. Data Preparation & Cleaning

Processed over 9,994 transactional records using Power Query. Handled missing delivery dates, filtered out redundant log entries, and structured proper data types to ensure high-integrity temporal calculations.

5. Data Modeling & DAX Implementation

Built a robust Star Schema architecture linking a Central Fact Table (Orders) with Dimension Tables (Customers, Products, Calendar, Geography). Authored custom DAX measures for Year-to-Date (YTD) sales, profit margins, and dynamic conditional formatting.

6. Dashboard Design & User Experience

Developed a clean, executive-ready multi-page dashboard featuring synchronized slicers, drill-through capabilities, and intuitive KPI cards for instant decision-making.

7. Key Insights Uncovered
  • Sub-categories like Tables and Bookcases showed negative net profit margins despite high aggregate revenue.
  • Standard Class shipping dominated over 60% of total volume, experiencing severe fulfillment bottlenecks during Q4 surges.
8. Client Solution & Business Impact

Delivered actionable recommendations to phase out loss-making discount thresholds on specific items and re-route regional logistics, enabling the client to optimize inventory expenditure and protect profit margins.
