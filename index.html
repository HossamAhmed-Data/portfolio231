Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

Power BI Dashboard & Case Study

Power BI Power Query DAX Excel Data Analysis
View Dashboard GitHub

01. Project Overview

An interactive sales and profitability dashboard created to understand performance across products, regions, customers, and sales categories.

02. Problem Statement

The project needed a clearer way to monitor sales and profitability and identify areas where strong sales did not necessarily result in strong profit.

03. Analytical Objective

Analyze sales and profit performance, identify underperforming areas, understand important trends, and discover factors affecting profitability.

04. Dataset

  • Transaction-level sales data
  • Product and category information
  • Customer and regional information
  • Sales, quantity, discount, and profit

05. Data Cleaning & Preparation

The dataset was cleaned and prepared using Power Query. The process included checking data types, handling missing values, removing unnecessary records, and preparing the data for analysis.

06. Data Modeling & Analysis

The data was organized into a structured analytical model. Relationships were created between relevant tables and DAX measures were used to calculate important metrics.

07. Dashboard

The dashboard provides an interactive overview of sales, profit, products, regions, and other important dimensions.

08. Key Insights

  • Identified products and categories with weaker profitability.
  • Compared sales and profit performance across different regions.
  • Analyzed how discounts can affect profitability.
  • Identified important sales trends and performance patterns.

09. Solution / Impact

The analysis helps identify profitability issues, monitor performance, and focus on products and regions that require further attention.
