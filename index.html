Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

Power BI Business Intelligence Dashboard

Power BI Power Query DAX Excel
View Dashboard View GitHub

01. Project Overview

An interactive sales and profitability dashboard designed to analyze business performance across products, regions, customers, and shipping categories.

02. Business Problem

The business needed a clearer view of sales performance and profitability. Management needed to identify areas generating strong sales but weak or negative profit.

03. Analytical Objective

The main objective was to understand sales and profit trends, identify underperforming products and regions, and discover the main factors affecting profitability.

04. Dataset

  • Transaction-level sales data
  • Product and category information
  • Customer and geographic information
  • Sales, quantity, discount, and profit

05. Data Cleaning & Preparation

The dataset was prepared using Power Query. The process included checking data types, handling missing values, removing unnecessary records, and preparing the data for analysis.

06. Data Modeling & Analysis

The data was organized into a structured analytical model and relationships were created between the main tables. DAX measures were then used to calculate important KPIs and business metrics.

07. Dashboard

The dashboard provides an interactive view of sales, profit, products, regions, and other important business dimensions.

08. Key Insights

  • Identified products and categories with weaker profitability.
  • Compared sales and profit performance across regions.
  • Analyzed the relationship between discounts and profitability.
  • Identified important sales trends and performance patterns.

09. Business Solution / Impact

The analysis helps management identify profitability issues, monitor sales performance, and focus on products, regions, and business areas that require further attention.
